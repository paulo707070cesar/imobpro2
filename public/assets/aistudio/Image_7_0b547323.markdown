## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, premium bio-tech and clinical interface that balances life-critical medical rigor with the tactile sophistication of luxury tech. Designed for discerning patients, clinical specialists, and executive healthcare stakeholders, the aesthetic projects profound authority, clinical quietude, and breakthrough technical mastery.

Drawing from modern minimalism fused with dark glassmorphism and surgical architectural precision:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep cosmic midnight tones provide an infinite visual stage where high-fidelity diagnostic data, imaging, and typography take precedence.
- **Luminous Precision:** Vibrant electric cyan accents cut through the deep obsidian foundation, guiding focus without visual clutter or unnecessary noise.
- **Architectural Discipline:** Structural low-radius contours (0.25rem to 0.75rem) avoid overly playful or toy-like roundness, reinforcing institutional trust and clinical exactness.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around deep optical absorption and targeted spectral luminance. 

- **Primary (`#00D2FF`):** A radiant cyan evoking laser imaging, neural firing, and high-frequency digital optics. Utilized exclusively for primary interactions, high-value status indicators, active states, and focal diagnostic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0070F3`):** An authoritative deep electric cobalt that grounds primary actions, anchors gradients, and balances high-energy cyan accents.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`):** A soft atmospheric sky tint deployed for secondary icons, informational badges, and muted glowing borders.
- **Neutral Base (`#030712` to `#0B1938`):** Surfaces leverage midnight slate and void black. Content tiers rise using translucent slate tints (`rgba(11, 25, 56, 0.7)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`).
- **Text & Accents:** High-clarity optical white (`#F8FAFC`) serves primary typographic hierarchy, while sub-labels step down gracefully into muted slate blues (`#94A3B8` and `#64748B`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the structured, geometric precision of Manrope for display and sectional headers with the neutral, low-contrast clarity of DM Sans for dense data tables, doctor bios, and body copy.

- **Uppercase Overlines:** Category headers, status chips, and section identifiers leverage `label-sm` with an expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`) rendered in primary cyan (`#00D2FF`) to establish clear clinical demarcations.
- **High-Contrast Headlines:** Major titles use bold weights with tight letter-spacing to command respect without resorting to heavy, cumbersome letterforms.
- **Micro-Copy Legibility:** Secondary body elements use `#94A3B8` against the deep canvas, preserving WCAG AAA legibility while ensuring primary titles and numbers dominate visual perception.
